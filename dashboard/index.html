How the implied value is priced

implied = TVL × multiple(category) × growth × maturity

  • multiple(category) — the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the tokenized protocols in that category. A category with fewer than 5 tokenized protocols uses the global median instead. Every multiple is clamped to 0.05–0.50.
  • growth — 1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%) ÷ 100 × 0.5.
  • maturity — 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4. A protocol with no listing date is priced as 365 days old.

Nothing here is a prediction that a token will launch, or when. The implied value is what the token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is — priced the way the market already prices tokens that trade.
